# Power BI Dashboard

## Purpose

The dashboard summarizes agrifood sales performance and helps identify opportunities for revenue and profit optimization.

## KPI cards

| KPI | Result |
|---|---:|
| Total Revenue | **845,320 MAD** |
| Total Profit | **212,450 MAD** |
| Profit Margin | **25.1%** |
| Total Units Sold | **18,750** |
| Avg Order Value | **45.1 MAD** |

## Analysis views

### Revenue and Profit by Category
Shows sales and profitability across Fruits, Vegetables, Dairy, Grains, Meat and Berries categories.

### Product Performance
Ranks products by revenue, profit, units sold and profit margin.

### Regional Performance
Compares sales and profit across Casablanca, Rabat, Marrakech, Fes, Tangier and other regions.

### Sales Channel Mix
Breaks down performance by Online, Retail, Supermarket and Wholesale channels.

### Monthly Trends
Tracks revenue, profit and units sold across January to December.

### Promotion Analysis
Compares promoted vs non-promoted orders on revenue, volume and margin.

## Dashboard design

The page follows a simple analytical flow:

**Executive Overview → Category/Product Analysis → Regional/Channel Analysis → Time Trends → Promotion Impact**

## Report areas

Recommended report areas include:
- Executive KPI overview
- Revenue and profit by category
- Product performance
- Regional performance
- Sales-channel mix
- Monthly trend analysis
- Promotion comparison
