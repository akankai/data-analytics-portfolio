# Analytical Findings — AgriFood Sales & Demand Analysis

## Dataset quality
The synthetic source dataset contains **1,500 sales transactions** and 12 fields.

- Missing values: **0**
- Duplicate rows: **0**
- Total revenue: **845,320 MAD**
- Total profit: **212,450 MAD**
- Overall profit margin: **25.1%**

## Category performance
Fruits category has the highest revenue (287,100 MAD) and profit (78,400 MAD), followed by Vegetables and Dairy.

## Product performance
Top revenue-generating products: Apples (98,200 MAD), Bananas (76,500 MAD), Carrots (65,300 MAD).
Top profit-generating products: Apples (24,100 MAD), Bananas (19,800 MAD), Strawberries (18,700 MAD).

## Regional performance
Casablanca region leads in revenue (215,400 MAD) and profit (54,200 MAD), followed by Rabat and Marrakech.

## Sales channel performance
Supermarket channel contributes the most revenue (312,500 MAD) and profit (82,100 MAD).
Online channel shows the highest average profit margin (28.5%) despite lower volume.

## Monthly trends
Q4 (Oct-Dec) shows peak sales driven by holiday promotions.
Q2 (Apr-Jun) has the lowest sales due to seasonal factors.

## Promotion impact
Promoted orders have 22% higher average order value but 15% lower profit margin compared to non-promoted orders.
Promotions drive volume but may reduce profitability if not carefully targeted.

## Key recommendations
1. Focus inventory and marketing efforts on high-margin products like Strawberries and Berries.
2. Expand successful Online channel strategies to other channels.
3. Target promotions to low-performing regions to boost demand without eroding margins.
4. Optimize seasonal product mix based on monthly demand patterns.
5. Monitor promotion effectiveness to balance volume growth with profit preservation.

## Scope and limitations
This project uses synthetic data created for portfolio and learning purposes. The findings demonstrate an analytics workflow and should not be interpreted as findings about a real agribusiness operation.
